I study at Xi’an Jiaotong-Liverpool University. My recent projects started with two questions. What happens to a video frame on its way to the screen? And how can I keep track of feedback after I get it?

Selected projects

Video over TCP and UDP

Python · OpenCV · Wireshark

I ran a video sender and receiver inside one Ubuntu virtual machine, then watched the clip at the receiving end. Capturing traffic on the loopback interface let me follow the transfer between the two programs.

What the frame counts tell me

The UDP receiver counted 27 fewer video frames than the sender in this run. A video frame can span several datagrams, so those two counters alone cannot tell me how many UDP datagrams were lost.

Feedback-to-Action Tracker

Flask · PostgreSQL

I built a web app that turns assessment comments into tasks. When I open a task, I can trace it back to the feedback that prompted it and see how its status has changed.

How I keep a task’s history

The task record holds its current status. A separate history table records the old status, the new status and the time of each change. That lets the dashboard show what is still open while the task page preserves earlier decisions.
